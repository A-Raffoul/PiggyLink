    How it works

    Spoken audio carries a second, encoded conversation. The nearby laptop decodes it.

    1. 01

      Open one role on each laptop. Place them side by side.

      Support laptop ↗Customer laptop ↗
    2. 02

      Start support, then customer. Allow microphone access.

    3. 03

      Toggle Encoded to show the hidden conversation.

    Deliberately trusting agents, fictional data. Encoded messages are not encrypted. Voice and transcription use online services.
