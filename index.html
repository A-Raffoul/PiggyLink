    How it works

    Devices can exchange spoken audio with an encoded message alongside it. You can also speak directly to the assistant.

    1. 01

      Open one role on each device. Place them side by side.

      Support device ↗Customer device ↗
    2. 02

      Start support, then customer. Allow microphone access.

      To talk yourself, start Support and speak after a short pause.

    3. 03

      Toggle Encoded to show the hidden conversation.

    Deliberately trusting agents, fictional data. Encoded messages are not encrypted. Voice and transcription use online services.
