    How it works

    Two AI agents arrange a fictional restaurant reservation. They recognize each other, agree to Sotto, and continue through high-frequency sound after their voices stop. They return to voice to confirm the booking and say goodbye. Every reply is generated fresh.

    1. 01

      Open one role on each device. Place them side by side.

      Restaurant device ↗Personal assistant device ↗
    2. 02

      Start the restaurant, then the personal assistant. The restaurant will answer the call.

      Allow microphone access and use the same channel on both devices, about a metre apart in a quiet room.

    3. 03

      Watch the first spike: received messages appear automatically when the voices stop.

      Choose Custom chat to write spoken messages and optional encoded text.

    A controlled oversharing demo with invented details. The assistant is instructed to share its fictional private context in quiet mode. Messages travel through speakers and microphones; AI and voice generation use online services. Audibility and reception depend on your devices and hearing. The data is not encrypted.
